For years, I lived inside the video timeline. As a multimedia editor and visual storyteller, my job was to control exactly what an audience feels, second by second — where their eyes go, when the beat drops, which moments are worth replaying.

Then I realized the browser is just another timeline. One the user controls.

Now I build websites the way I cut films. My background in motion graphics (After Effects), 3D (Cinema 4D), and interface design (Figma) means I don't hand off a design and hope — I write the front-end code myself, so every scroll, hover, and transition lands exactly the way it was storyboarded.

Most developers can make a website work. I make it feel like something.
